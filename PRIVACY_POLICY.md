# Privacy Policy & Data Deletion - Konuşma

Last Updated: May 30, 2026

## 1. Data Collection
We collect your email address and name solely for the purpose of maintaining your learning progress, streaks, and "Survival Kit" favorites. This data is essential for cross-device synchronization and academic progress tracking.

## 2. Account Deletion Request
You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. We believe in complete ownership of your personal learning journey.

### How to Request Deletion:
To delete your account, please send an email to: **booodekya13@gmail.com**
- **Subject**: Account Deletion Request - Konuşma
- **Required Info**: Please provide the email address used for the account to verify ownership.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
Once a deletion request is received:
- Your account, email, and name will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.
- All offline progress, streaks, and favorites stored on your device will be removed when you uninstall the app.
- We do not keep any personal data after the account is deleted.
- No data is sold or shared with third-party advertising entities.

*This policy ensures compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global data protection standards.*
